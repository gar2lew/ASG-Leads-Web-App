# Ideas

## Purpose

This file captures ideas before they become prioritised backlog items. It keeps useful product thinking visible without turning every idea into committed scope.

## Idea Intake Rules

- Capture ideas quickly, then prioritise later.
- Keep ideas specific to ASG CRM, ASG-X, staff workflow, reporting, integrations, or client operations.
- Do not treat an idea as approved scope until it is promoted into `docs/FEATURE_BACKLOG.md`.
- Do not build AI, Salestrail, Firebase, or data automation ideas without safety review.
- Prefer version-based planning from this point forward.

## Promotion Criteria Into FEATURE_BACKLOG.md

- Defined user group.
- Expected workflow.
- Known dependencies.
- Risk level.
- Target release candidate.
- Validation approach.
